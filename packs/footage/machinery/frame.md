# OpsRamp reel system

## Concept

An editorial film frame with product evidence inside it. The frame makes one
plain-language point; a real OpsRamp Digital Twin capture proves that point.

## Frame

- Landscape, 1920 by 1080, at 30 frames per second.
- Keep important copy inside an 88-pixel edge.
- Reserve the lowest 160 pixels for player captions.
- Use deep forest for major ideas and warm canvas for explanation.
- Use green for the active relationship. Amber means a real boundary or risk.
- Product windows use simple browser chrome and a factual status strip.

## Typography

HPE Graphik is bundled locally. Headlines are large, sentence case, and short.
Body copy is never a transcript. Small labels use tracking and tabular figures.

## Composition

- One dominant idea per frame.
- A product-in-perspective proof frame places the point on the left and a large
  authentic product capture on the right.
- Straight-on product proof is used whenever the audience must read the UI.
- Diagrams animate the relationship being explained.
- Every frame carries a small source or status label when omission could create
  a false impression.

## Motion

Use restrained entrances, border traces, route traces, focus shifts, and a
0.44-second dissolve between chapters. Motion follows meaning; it does not fill
silence. Holds are allowed, but the scene progress line keeps the long-form
timeline visibly alive.

## Do not

- Do not fabricate telemetry, customer outcomes, or product status.
- Do not turn evidence into a feature tour.
- Do not use decorative gradients in type.
- Do not use spinning icons, bouncing controls, or constant camera zoom.
- Do not publish internal footage through Hyperframes cloud paths.
